Privacy Policy

In short: Overlay Pencil doesn't collect any data. The picture and your drawing travel only across your local network, between your iPad and your computer.

Effective October 31, 2026. Covers Overlay Pencil for iPad and Overlay Pencil Remote for Mac.

Who publishes the apps

NowakProduction. Contact: nowakproduction@gmail.com.

What data we collect

None. The apps have no accounts and no ads, use no analytics and don't track you. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

What happens to the picture and the drawing

The picture from OBS, the drawing layer and playback commands go directly between your devices on the local network, over NDI® and the connection to Overlay Pencil Remote. They don't go to the internet or to our servers — we don't have any.

Purchases

Premium is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store whether Premium is unlocked on your Apple ID, and remembers the answer on your device.

Permissions the apps ask for, and why

Local Network — iPad and Mac

To find OBS on your network, receive its picture, send the drawing layer back, and connect the iPad to Overlay Pencil Remote.

Add to Photos — iPad

To save to your photo library only the frames you choose when reviewing snapshots. The app can't read your photos.

Accessibility — Mac, Overlay Pencil Remote only

To send the player's keyboard shortcuts — pause and skip — to the app playing the video. It doesn't read what you type or what's on your screen.

What stays on your device

Settings such as brush colour and width, the last source, the pairing PIN, and snapshots waiting for review are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply. We don't share them with anyone.

Children

The apps don't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
