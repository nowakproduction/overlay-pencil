Overlay Pencil Remote for Mac

Pause and skip a video on your computer right from the iPad, without walking to the keyboard. Works with YouTube in a browser, VLC, QuickTime and any player that uses the same shortcuts.

Download Overlay Pencil Remote

Free · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon or Intel · signed and notarized by Apple

Install

  1. Open the downloaded ZIP file and drag Overlay Pencil Remote to your Applications folder.
  2. Open the app. It has no window — just a ⏯ icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
  3. Grant Accessibility permission. Without it, the app can't send keyboard shortcuts to the player. macOS asks the first time you open it. If you missed it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and turn on Overlay Pencil Remote. The ⏯ menu then shows “Zgoda Dostępność: przyznana” (permission granted).
  4. Allow local network access when macOS asks. That's how the iPad finds the computer.
  5. Pair the iPad. Read the six-digit PIN in the ⏯ menu. On the iPad, open Overlay Pencil → gear icon → Computer Control and enter the PIN. Once connected, the toolbar under the picture shows −10, −5, play/pause, +5 and +10.

The Mac app's menu is in Polish; English translations of its labels are given in brackets above.

How it works

The iPad sends a command, and Overlay Pencil Remote presses the player's shortcut on the computer: K pauses, J and L skip 10 seconds, and the ← → arrows skip 5 seconds.

By default the shortcuts go to the app in front. If you're working in something else on the computer, pick the app with the video in the ⏯ menu → Steruj aplikacją (Control app). The shortcuts then reach it even when it isn't in front.

A browser video in OBS

If OBS captures a browser window with YouTube and you control it from the iPad, the browser isn't the active window. Chrome may then slow down rendering and YouTube may lower the quality. Two things prevent that:

  • Set YouTube's quality manually: gear icon → Quality → 1080p instead of “Auto”.
  • Quit Chrome completely (⌘Q) and start it from Terminal like this:
open -a "Google Chrome" --args --disable-backgrounding-occluded-windows --disable-renderer-backgrounding

Uninstall

Quit the app from the ⏯ menu and move it from Applications to the Trash. You can remove its entry in Privacy & Security → Accessibility with the “−” button.
